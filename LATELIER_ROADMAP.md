# L’Atelier · Long-Term Product Roadmap

## Vision
Transform **L’Atelier** into the world’s most comprehensive, scientifically informed, and visually elegant culinary platform—bridging high-end gastronomic academy education, restaurant brigade line efficiency, home kitchen accessibility, and molecular food physics.

---

## Pillar 1: Recipe Encyclopedia (Target: 1,000+ Master Recipes)
- [x] Phase 1: Establish strict 28-field schema (metric weights, sensory cues, science notes, troubleshooting).
- [x] Phase 2: Reach 100+ baseline master recipes across global cuisines.
- [x] Phase 3: Reach 150+ master recipes with expansive dessert/pâtisserie repertoire (43 desserts: soufflés, cheesecakes, tarts, gelato, sorbets, entremets, macarons, mille-feuille, éclairs).
- [ ] Phase 4: Expand to 250+ recipes: Regional Italian hyper-local pastas (Amatriciana, Carbonara with guanciale, Cacio e Pepe mantecatura, Agnolotti del Plin), Mexican Mole Poblano & Mole Negro, French Charcuterie (Terrines, Pâté en Croûte, Duck Confit).
- [ ] Phase 5: Expand to 500+ recipes: Whole animal butchery applications, artisan cheese-making, charcuterie curing, fermentation masters (Koji, Garum, Tempeh, Miso, Black Garlic).
- [ ] Phase 6: Extensible community/user recipe importer with automated nutritional and sensory analysis.

---

## Pillar 2: Professional Brigade Operations & Manuals
- [x] Phase 1: Distill 12 authentic restaurant manuals into standardized digital format (`data-prep-manual.js`).
- [x] Phase 2: Build dedicated Kitchen Prep Manual view with station filters, real-time search, dynamic batch scaling, and HACCP critical limits.
- [x] Phase 3: Connect batch ingredients to Provisions shopping list.
- [ ] Phase 4: Line Station Map: Interactive graphical layout of hot line stations (Sauté, Grill, Broiler, Fry, Garde Manger, Pastry).
- [ ] Phase 5: Waste tracking and yield loss calculator (EP vs. AP percentages for proteins and produce).
- [ ] Phase 6: Temperature logging journal for HACCP compliance inspections.

---

## Pillar 3: Food Science & Molecular Gastronomy
- [x] Phase 1: Interactive thermodynamic simulations (Heat transfer kinetics, Maillard curve, emulsion physics, collagen hydrolysis).
- [x] Phase 2: Hydrocolloid Dosing Calculator (Agar, Sodium Alginate, Xanthan, Ultratex).
- [x] Phase 3: Botanical and wild foraging field guide with toxicity warnings.
- [ ] Phase 4: Fluid Gel Viscosity Simulator: dynamic shear-rate curve generator based on gellan/agar concentration.
- [ ] Phase 5: Transglutaminase (Meat Glue) enzymatic bonding guide and cross-linking protocols.
- [ ] Phase 6: Rotary evaporator & vacuum distillation aroma extraction guide.
