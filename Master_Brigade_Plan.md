## II. BACKWARD-SCHEDULED CRITICAL PATH TIMELINE

### 04:30 PM (T-180m) — Course 01: Salmon Cornets with Sweet Red Onion Crème Fraîche
- **Assigned Brigade Station:** `Garde Manger` (Cold Larder, Crudos, Caviar & Vinaigrettes)
- **Execution Protocol:** Bake tuile cones at 175°C. Roll immediately on metal cornet molds. Fill with onion crème fraîche and diced salmon.
- **Plating Blueprint:** Present upright in black sea salt within a chilled marble block.
- **HACCP & Quality Storage:** Hold prepped components at <=4.0°C; hot-hold emulsions at 60°C in bain-marie.

### 05:30 PM (T-120m) — Course 02: Oysters and Pearls (Tapioca Sabayon & Caviar)
- **Assigned Brigade Station:** `Poissonnier / Entremetier` (Seafood Poaching, Searing & Silky Purées)
- **Execution Protocol:** Whisk egg yolks with reduced oyster liquor over double boiler until thick sabayon forms. Fold in tapioca. Gently poach oysters 40 seconds.
- **Plating Blueprint:** Spoon warm sabayon, rest poached oysters in center, crown with caviar.
- **HACCP & Quality Storage:** Hold prepped components at <=4.0°C; hot-hold emulsions at 60°C in bain-marie.

### 06:15 PM (T-75m) — Course 03: Lavender & Honey Glazed Duck with Spiced Cherry Gastrique
- **Assigned Brigade Station:** `Grillardin / Saucier` (High-Heat Maillard Proteins & Demi-Glace/Gastriques)
- **Execution Protocol:** Score duck skin. Render on medium-low in cold dry pan for 9 minutes. Flip and cook 2 minutes to 54°C core. Rest 8 minutes before slicing.
- **Plating Blueprint:** Carve medallions skin-up. Arc warm cherry gastrique beside protein.
- **HACCP & Quality Storage:** Hold prepped components at <=4.0°C; hot-hold emulsions at 60°C in bain-marie.

### 07:00 PM (T-30m) — Course 04: Aubergine couscous salad
- **Assigned Brigade Station:** `Pâtissier` (Sub-Zero Granitas, Sorbets & Hydrosols)
- **Execution Protocol:** step 1
Heat grill to high. Put the aubergine on a baking sheet, brush with oil and season. Grill for about 15 mins, turning and brushing with more oil halfway, until browned and softened.

step 2
Meanwhile, tip the couscous into a large bowl, pour over the stock, then cover and leave for 10 mins. Mix the tomatoes, mint, goat’s cheese and remaining oil together. Fluff the couscous up with a fork, then stir in the aubergines, tomato mixture and lemon juice.
- **Plating Blueprint:** Present on heated/chilled porcelain with clean lines and sauce nappe.
- **HACCP & Quality Storage:** Hold prepped components at <=4.0°C; hot-hold emulsions at 60°C in bain-marie.

### 07:20 PM (T-10m) — Course 05: Oops! I Dropped the Lemon Tart (Shattered Limoncello Sabayon)
- **Assigned Brigade Station:** `Pâtissier` (Custards, Ganaches, Soufflés & Crisp Sablés)
- **Execution Protocol:** Whip lemon curd with limoncello. Break baked sablé crust into rustic shards. Pipe fluid gel.
- **Plating Blueprint:** Splat curd deliberately across asymmetric black plate. Scatter crust shards and candied capers.
- **HACCP & Quality Storage:** Hold prepped components at <=4.0°C; hot-hold emulsions at 60°C in bain-marie.
